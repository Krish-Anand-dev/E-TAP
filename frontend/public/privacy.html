E-TAP Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

E-TAP checks suspicious emails and messages and explains, in plain language, whether they look dangerous. This page says what happens to the things you send us, on the website and through our WhatsApp number. It is written to be read, not skimmed past.

What we receive

  • On the website: the email file (.eml or .msg), pasted message text or files you choose to upload.
  • On WhatsApp: the text, links, images and documents you send or forward to the E-TAP WhatsApp number (up to 5 files, 25 MB each).
  • Your WhatsApp number, which WhatsApp gives us with each message. We use it to limit how many messages one number can send per hour (kept in the server's memory only) and, if you choose to link it to your account, to show your forwarded messages under My reports. A linked number is stored only as a one-way keyed hash, never as the number itself, and is never saved in a report. You can unlink it any time in Settings.
  • If you sign in (optional, with an emailed code): your email address, and short-lived hashed sign-in codes and sessions. Signing in is never required to check a message.

What we do with it

We run an automated check and send back a result. E-TAP looks at the sender details, links, attachments (file type, hashes, macros, text inside images and QR codes), and asks an AI model to explain the evidence. By default the check is static: files are inspected but never opened or run. We do not use your messages for advertising, we do not sell them, and we do not build profiles of people.

What is saved, and for how long

  • A report: the verdict and score, the evidence behind it, and the links, domains and file fingerprints found. Before message text is saved, we mask card numbers, phone numbers, Aadhaar, PAN and SSN numbers, and email addresses in the body.
  • Images found in a message (screenshots, QR codes) may be stored with our image host so the report can show them. They have a random, unguessable address and stay blurred until clicked.
  • Reports are kept for 30 days by default. You can choose 7 to 365 days under Settings on the website. After that they are deleted.

Who else processes it

To do the check we use these services. Each receives only what its job needs:

  • VirusTotal (links and file fingerprints) and AbuseIPDB, WHOIS and ip-api (IP addresses and domains). Links that carry an email address, token or other secret are shortened to the plain address or not sent at all.
  • Google Gemini, for the AI explanation of the (masked) message content.
  • Cloudinary, to store images found in messages.
  • MongoDB Atlas (database), Render (server) and Vercel (website).
  • Meta / WhatsApp, which carries your WhatsApp messages. Its own privacy policy applies to your use of WhatsApp.

These providers have their own terms, which apply to the data they handle.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a report: open the report link we sent you (or find it under Reports), open Sharing, retention & deletion and choose Delete investigation. This deletes the report and its stored images.
  • Ask us to delete it: open an issue at github.com/Krish-Anand-dev/E-TAP/issues with the report link (do not post the message itself). Your WhatsApp number is not saved with the report (only a hash, if you linked it to an account), so the link is how we find it.
  • Sign-in data: you can sign out at any time in Settings, and sessions expire after 30 days.

Security

Traffic uses HTTPS. Requests from WhatsApp are verified with a secret signature before we act on them. No automated system is perfectly secure, so please do not send us anything you are not comfortable having analysed by the services above.

Children

E-TAP is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/Krish-Anand-dev/E-TAP/issues.
